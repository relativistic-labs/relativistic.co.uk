The field: reference implementation

001 / UNBOUND Move your pointer through the field. In GRANTED, nothing leaves the cone.

Notes for the WebGL2 port

The maths lives in field.js. Every formation is a closed-form function of a particle's fixed attributes (u, angle, seed, size, signal) and time, so it moves into a vertex shader as written. Uniforms: time, the two formations being blended, the blend factor, the pointer, the cone weight and the theme colours.

Brief details implemented here: four formations of 9 seconds with a smootherstep blend; about 1% signal particles, which in GRANTED sit on the cone's boundary; the pointer clamp that keeps particles inside the cone; pale particles with an accent glow at the apex on ink, ink dots with density on paper; colours read from the theme's custom properties; reduced motion draws one still of GRANTED; forced colours hide the canvas.
